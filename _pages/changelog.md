# Changelog

## **Version 1.0.0**

ReturnNotice is live on the App Store.

#### New

- Automatic return-by dates based on each store's policy, your region and your memberships.
- Live countdowns with smart home sections: Overdue, This Week, In 14 Days and Later.
- Return-window reminders, custom reminders and a Pro daily digest.
- AI Scan for receipt photos and product links — processed entirely on device.
- Attach photos and PDF receipts to any return.
- Home-screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets and Live Activity countdowns.
- Apple Watch app and Mac app.
- Calendar integration and an insights dashboard.
- Optional iCloud sync across your devices.
- Backup, restore and export of your data.
