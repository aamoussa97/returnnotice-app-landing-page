## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does ReturnNotice know my return deadline?**

The app comes with built-in return policies for popular stores and applies the policy matching each store and your region. Store memberships that extend return windows (e.g. Prime) are taken into account automatically.

**What is AI Scan?**

AI Scan lets you snap a photo of a receipt or paste a product link, and the app extracts the item details for you. All processing happens entirely on your device — your photos and data never leave it.

**Is my data private?**

Yes. ReturnNotice is local-first: your data stays on your device unless you enable optional iCloud sync via your own Apple ID. The app contains no analytics, ads or trackers. See the [Privacy Policy](https://aamoussa97.github.io/returnnotice-app-landing-page/privacypolicy) for details.

**What does ReturnNotice Pro include?**

Pro unlocks unlimited active returns (the free plan includes 3), calendar integration, custom categories, the Apple Watch app and AI Scan for receipts and links — all via a one-time purchase.
